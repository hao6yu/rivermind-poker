# RiverMind Poker support

Submit product feedback from **Profile → Send feedback** so RiverMind can safely include its build and bounded diagnostic context. For private support, privacy questions, or deletion help, email [hyu@isw.dev](mailto:hyu@isw.dev?subject=RiverMind%20Poker%20support).

When using email, please include:

- the RiverMind version and build shown in **Profile → Privacy & support**;
- your iPhone or iPad model and iOS/iPadOS version;
- the screen and action where the problem occurred; and
- a screenshot when it does not expose information you want to keep private.

Do not post account identifiers, private hand history, or other personal information in a public GitHub issue. Use the in-app form or email for anything private.

The RiverMind privacy policy is available in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). RiverMind uses practice chips only and does not support real-money wagering.
