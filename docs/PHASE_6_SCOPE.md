# Phase 6 — Applied Personal Coaching

Status: complete.

## Goal

Connect real gameplay, hand review, and training into one useful local-first
coaching loop. Phase 6 should make the app feel more personal without requiring
an account or cross-device history.

## Slice 1 — Smarter post-game coaching (implemented)

- Turn graded session decisions into up to two evidence-based strengths and one
  priority focus.
- Keep the strength and focus labels distinct; sparse sessions must not invent
  confidence.
- Link the priority focus to its most useful hand replay and matching practice.
- Keep the recap compact and readable on small phone screens.

## Slice 2 — Personal practice plan (implemented)

- Build a short next-session plan from recurring leaks, spaced review, and
  incomplete learning activities.
- Explain why each activity is recommended and let the learner start it in one
  tap.
- Update the plan using only locally stored progress and play history.

## Slice 3 — Learn-screen simplification (implemented)

- Give the personal plan clear priority over the full content catalog.
- Make resume, recommended next, and browse-all states immediately distinct.
- Preserve access to every lesson and practice pack without crowding the first
  screen.

## Slice 4 — Optional landscape six-player table (implemented)

- Offer an in-game landscape layout for six-player sessions where it materially
  improves seat and board readability.
- Preserve portrait play and never force orientation outside the table.
- Validate rotation, safe areas, action controls, and modal transitions on
  common phone sizes.

## Slice 5 — Progress insights and polish (implemented)

- Show useful trends such as improving concepts and recurring review areas
  without implying solver-perfect precision.
- Tighten loading, empty, accessibility, and reduced-motion states across the
  coaching loop.
- Complete the small-phone, localization, and full release gates.

## Deferred

- Accounts and cross-device history sync
- Friends, realtime tables, leaderboards, and social sharing
- Push reminders
- Additional poker variants
- Large curriculum expansion before the personal loop is validated

## Phase acceptance

- A completed session produces an honest, actionable recap from its decisions.
- The learner can move from a leak to the relevant hand and practice in one tap.
- The Learn screen presents a concise personal plan while retaining the catalog.
- Six-player gameplay is comfortable in portrait and optionally landscape.
- English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese remain complete.
- The full automated suite and compact-phone release gate pass.
