## Locale architecture

- `src/localization/registry.ts`: `ja` added to `AppLanguage` with
  `displayName: '日本語'`, `displayNameKey: 'language.ja'`,
  `intlLocale: 'ja-JP'`, `nativeLocales: ['ja']`,
  `storeLocales: { appStore: 'ja', googlePlay: 'ja-JP' }` (App Store Connect's
  Japanese metadata id), `aiCoachSupported: true`, `catalogComplete: true`,
  `releaseEnabled: false`. `resolveLanguageFromLocales` maps every `ja-*`
  system locale to Japanese only while release-enabled (English while
  draft-gated); `normalizeLanguagePreference` sanitizes saved `ja`
  preferences in production like the other drafts.
- `plurals.ts`: `japanesePlurals` is empty **by design** — Japanese has no
  plural inflection; count templates render the base with `{{count}}` for
  0/1/2+ and the contextual counter lives in the string (人, 枚, 件, 回, 分,
  問, ハンド, アウツ). An empty catalog also guarantees Japanese can never
  select an English plural form (lookup is per-locale).
- `format.ts`: `localizedOrdinalPlace` renders `1位` for ja.
- `scenarioCatalog.ts`: `ScenarioVocab` gained the optional `handJoiner`
  (locale-neutral generalization; ja joins `9-9のペア` directly, Latin locales
  default to the space) — no screen branches on locale identity.
- Native declarations: `app.json` `supportedLocales` gains `ja` on both
  platforms; `verify-native-locales.mjs` and `verify-release-config.mjs`
  expected-locale lists extended to six.

## Glossary decisions and retained-term allowlist

- Actions in katakana: フォールド/チェック/コール/ベット/レイズ/オールイン;
  raise-to renders `{{amount}}ビッグブラインドにレイズ`; all-in variants per
  guide §3 (オールインにコール/オールインにレイズ).
- Streets katakana: プリフロップ/フロップ/ターン/リバー/ボード/ショーダウン
  (the es/pt Latin-street retention was deliberately NOT copied).
- Retained Latin (guide §4): 3-bet, 4-bet, C-bet, SPR, EV, ICM, MTT, UTG, MP,
  CO, BTN, SB, `BB` (compact amounts + the one `guide.bb` seat label —
  moneyUnits gate proves it is the only `BB` in the catalog), `Sit & Go`, `K`
  (1.2K), RiverMind, AI, Lv. Never 3ベット/三ベット, never プレフロップ.
- Product terms: 学習パス, プラクティスパック, シナリオトレーニング,
  ハンドランキング, チートシート, 初期設定, ハンド履歴, デイリーチャレンジ,
  チャンピオンシップ, クイックプレイ, プライベートテーブル.
- Counters fixed per family (guide §6); half-width digits; `,`/`.` poker
  notation invariant; full-width ？！、。「」; no spaces between numerals and
  particles (40にレイズ, 残り2人); no furigana.
- `{{heroHand}}` renders `A-5のスート` / `9-9のペア` via the new `handJoiner`
  contract field.

## Defects found and dispositions

1. **Inventory generator latent breakage (Phase 19 residue).**
   `generate-localization-inventory.mjs` could not evaluate the composed
   `englishMessages` (Phase 19 spread composition) — fixed by extracting the
   unexported `baseEnglishMessagesInternal` literal and recomposing the
   surface; the marker now accepts module-private `const` declarations. The
   frozen Phase 19 inventory file was not regenerated (still the `c807082d`
   snapshot by design).
2. **Throwaway checker bugs.** The `.claude/tmp` slice checker referenced an
   out-of-scope variable for the identical-value allowlist, mishandled the
   basename dot in full-key matching, and wrongly banned 完成/成功 (valid
   Japanese; the ban existed to catch Chinese leakage). All fixed. The over-
   broad ban caused a handful of agents to reword natural 完成 phrasings
   (できあがる/完了 variants) — harmless draft variance flagged for the native
   reviewer.
3. **Scenario-2 math-contract deviation.** The ja agent emitted
   `{mathFallback}` reasoning objects for four math templates (es/pt ship
   plain-string reasoning there) and hardcoded instance numbers (8/40/20%/28%,
   30/84/36%/20%) in two of them. Converted to plain strings that mirror the
   es placeholder/literal policy per template; the full es-vs-ja placeholder
   parity audit across all 81 templates × every field reports **0
   deviations**.
4. **Language self-name convention.** The base-1-core agent first translated
   `language.es419`/`language.ptBr` into Japanese glosses; restored glosses
   (`スペイン語（ラテンアメリカ）`/`ポルトガル語（ブラジル）`) to match the
   es/pt cross-gloss convention after the sync allowlist analysis; `language.ja`
   added to every catalog (typed `displayNameKey` parity) — an intentional
   shared, additive change.
5. **`table.insight.points` kept Latin "pt"** — fixed to `{{value}}点`
   (matching the es/pt translation of the same key); quality-gate regex also
   corrected to allow required full-width ？！ punctuation while still banning
   full-width alphanumerics.
