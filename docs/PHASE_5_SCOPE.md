# Phase 5 — Training Depth & Coaching Quality

Status: complete.

## Goal

Give RiverMind enough intermediate material to keep a learner improving after
the beginner path, while making every practice decision easier to understand on
a small phone. Phase 5 deepens the local-first learning product; it does not add
accounts or social dependencies.

## Slice 1 — Three-bet decisions (implemented)

- Preserve the completed beginner preflop path and add a clearly labeled
  intermediate section after its mastery check.
- Teach how to construct a three-bet, call, four-bet, and fold plan from value,
  blockers, position, sizing, and effective stack depth.
- Add a dedicated three-bet practice pack with at least eight independently
  randomized decision families.
- Identify the reason a selected action succeeds or fails before explaining the
  preferred baseline.
- Feed the new lesson and practice evidence into adaptive recommendations.

## Slice 2 — Postflop range interaction (implemented)

- Expand board-texture work into range advantage and nut advantage.
- Add single-raised and three-bet-pot flop decisions with heads-up and multiway
  adjustments.
- Teach turn barreling around equity changes, blockers, and likely folds.
- Add dedicated practice that avoids recently encountered scenario families.

## Slice 3 — River decisions and session recap (implemented)

- Deepen thin value, polarized sizing, bluff selection, and bluff catching.
- End a focused session with two observed strengths and one recommended focus.
- Link the focus directly to the matching lesson, practice pack, or spaced
  review queue.

## Content and UX quality bar

- Every new scenario declares its difficulty and a stable concept family.
- Every incorrect option explains the underlying issue, such as range,
  position, sizing, stack depth, or commitment.
- English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese remain complete.
- Long explanations remain readable at 375-point width and iPhone SE height.
- New content is deterministic, testable, and does not claim solver-perfect
  strategy.
- Existing learning progress and review history continue without migration loss.

## Deferred

- User-facing accounts and cross-device history sync
- Friends, realtime tables, and leaderboards
- Push reminders
- Nine-player tables and additional poker variants
- Monetization gates around unvalidated curriculum content

## Phase acceptance

- A learner who completed Phase 4 receives an intermediate next step rather
  than being sent back through completed beginner material.
- Each new concept connects teaching, randomized practice, adaptive
  recommendation, and spaced review.
- Practice sessions vary cards, positions, stack depths, and action sizes—not
  only question order.
- Coaching explains both the selected action and the preferred baseline.
- The full automated suite, localization coverage, and small-phone release gate
  pass before Phase 5 is marked complete.
